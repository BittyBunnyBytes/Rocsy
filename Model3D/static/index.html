Make a model

Keeps the shape and paints it. Add a picture of the object from the front, or leave the picture empty and describe the colours below: the Image studio then paints over a front view of your model.

The Image studio draws the picture first (its own GPU turn), then it becomes a 3D model.

    Physics for

    Pick the loose parts that should swing on their own. They get their own bones, the motion is simulated against every animation and baked in, and the file also carries VRM spring bones for engines that simulate live.

    Painting goes through the model, so brushing the front of a skirt also picks its back.

    Fine-tune (all parts)

    Rig

    Adds a skeleton, skin weights and preview animations. The plain model is kept, so you can re-rig any time.

    Edit

    Hunyuan3D can't edit a mesh directly, so the Image studio (Qwen Image 2.1) edits the model's picture from your instruction, and the model is made from the edited picture.
