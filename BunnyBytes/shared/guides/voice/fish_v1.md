# Fish Audio 1.5 — script guide

The open-source Fish Speech 1.5 engine: voice cloning from the character's
reference clip. Its documentation lists no emotion or tone markers.

## Quick syntax
- Plain spoken text only — bracket or parenthesis tags are not supported and may be read aloud.
- Languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, French, German, Arabic, Spanish; mixed-language text is fine.
- Punctuation controls pauses and intonation.
- Spell out numbers, symbols and abbreviations.
- For whispering, laughing or emotion cues, switch to Fish Audio S2 Pro.

## Details for Hermes
- Convey emotion through wording and punctuation only.
- Return only the words to be spoken.

Sources: github.com/fishaudio/fish-speech (v1.5.1 README); docs.fish.audio (S1 parenthesis tags are a different, cloud-only model).
