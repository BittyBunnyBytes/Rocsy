GPUs
Detected cards

In ROCm order (PCI bus order): "GPU 0" is ROCm0. Each card is kept by its ROCm UUID, so adding a card or moving one to another slot doesn't change which work runs where. The PCIe link is the narrowest hop between the card and the CPU.

Where each kind of work runs

Default = the primary card (the one with the most VRAM), or every card with the primary first for the multi-GPU roles. ComfyUI and Voice pick a change up the next time they start (Restart ComfyUI below), the LLM on its next model load. A card that is missing falls back to the default.
